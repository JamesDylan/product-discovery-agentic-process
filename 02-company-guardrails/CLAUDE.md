## Questions in scope
1. How do we give people confidence in a company's preferences and policy, even before either has been set up?
2. How do we spot the right moment to introduce customers to features they haven't adopted?

## Run-specific notes by stage

**`02_explore` — split the two questions.** Q1 (confidence before setup) is aspirational — B4B has
no equivalent today, so lean hard on analogues outside travel: default-safe settings in consumer
finance, trust signals before account setup, progressive disclosure of policy. Q2 (right moment to
introduce a feature) is empirical — lean on existing behavioural and adoption data, not analogy.

**`05_pressure-test` — the exposure that matters here.** This is company money and duty of care.
Test where a confidence signal shown before policy exists could mislead a traveller, and where a
well-timed prompt becomes nagging or manipulation.
