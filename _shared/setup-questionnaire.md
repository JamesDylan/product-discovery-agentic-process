# Setup questionnaire — configure the factory once

Fills `b4b-context.md`. Answer fast and roughly; mark anything uncertain `[assumption]`.
A rough answer in the file beats a perfect one in someone's head.

1. What is B4B, in two sentences, to someone who has never seen it?
2. What are the three jobs it does well today, and the one it does badly?
3. Who administers, who books, who pays — and are they the same person?
4. Give me every current number you have: acceptance rate, formation rate, adoption gaps, churn.
5. Where does the money come from, and what actually moves it?
6. What does Booking.com care about that we don't talk about internally?
7. Which constraints are genuinely technical and which are habit?
8. What research exists, how old is it, and can you open it right now?
9. Who are the three SMEs whose 20 minutes would save a day?
10. What is Serko saying externally about AI, and where does B4B sit in that story?
11. What has this team already tried in this space that didn't work?

Stop when the answers stop changing what a stage would do.
